# Purlay — Product glossary (`GLOSSARY.md`)

The words the product uses for its own things, in English and Turkish. Every client — the web client, the native
Apple apps and any later one — uses these words, in its own interface and in what it tells people. This repository
owns the list (D-359); a client that needs a term it does not have adds it here first.

A term's **form** matters as much as its word: where a string is a button, a title or a sentence, it follows the
copy rules of D-348 (short strings end without a period, buttons keep the bare verb).

## Address form

| Language | Rule |
|---|---|
| Turkish | **siz** everywhere (D-350). A command on a control keeps the bare verb (*Kaydet*, *Oturum aç*); everything said to the person is *siz* (*oturum açın*, *yapabilirsiniz*) and *your* is *-iniz* (*Profiliniz*). *Sen* forms are not used. |
| English | Second person, plain: *you*, *your*. British spelling and typographic apostrophes (D-351). |
