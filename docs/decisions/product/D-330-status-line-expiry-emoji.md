# D-330. A status line clears itself at the end chosen, and may carry one emoji — builds on D-108, D-329, amends D-131

**Decided under the owner's standing instruction to decide and proceed**, from the request for a status that clears itself (Teams and Discord: "Clear after" 30 minutes to a week, and an emoji beside it). A status line had no end: "In a meeting" and "Lunch" stayed on profiles for days, and the native clients could show a status but had no endpoint in their bundle to set one.

1. **The contract.** `UpdateProfileRequest` takes `statusEmoji` (one Unicode emoji, the `emoji` format a reaction already has: a flag, a keycap, a toned or joined pictograph; no shortcodes — custom emoji are a non-goal, D-4) and `statusUntil` (ahead, at most `STATUS_UNTIL_MAX_DAYS` = 7 days, so "this week" fits; the shared rule of D-329, refused by name on `statusUntil`). `Profile` returns both, optional because an older server never sends them. `PATCH /api/me/profile` is now in the endpoint table as `updateProfile`, so the native bundle can set a status beside `setAway`.
2. **The line is written whole.** A request naming any of `status`, `statusEmoji` and `statusUntil` sets all three, one left out cleared. Yesterday's client sends `status` alone and gets what it always got, a line for good — never an old end that clears its new words an hour later. A title or a zone changed alone leaves the line as it is. An end with neither words nor an emoji has nothing to clear and is not kept.
3. **It clears by the server's clock, read, not run** — Deep Work's rule (D-108), and the timed level’s (D-329). `users.status_emoji` and `users.status_until` (migration `0123`) are stored; every read (`/api/me`, the members, a member changed or converted) answers a line past its end as cleared — words, emoji and end all null. Nothing runs at the instant, so no frame announces it: a client that holds a line also holds its end, and stops drawing it then (the web compares with its minute); a list read after the end is already right. A change made by the person is still announced as before (`WORKSPACE_UPDATED`, D-131). The stored row keeps the old line until the person writes again; the export and the erasure carry and clear the two columns with the words.
4. **The web.** Profile in Settings has *Status emoji* and *Clear after*: don't clear, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, *Today* (the viewer's next midnight) and *This week* (the midnight that begins next Monday), on this device's clock and zone; a line that already clears offers *Until 13:00* first and keeps that end when only its words or emoji change. The profile panel shows the emoji, the words and *Until 13:00* (the date when not today); the conversation heading shows the emoji and the words.

**Rejected.** A job that clears expired lines and announces each: a line would outlive its end by the job's period, and the job would be one more thing to be overdue (D-108's argument). An emoji shortcode (`:palm_tree:`): a second vocabulary every client must map, for no glyph Unicode lacks. Keeping the end when only the words are sent: an old client would set "Back" and see it vanish at the end of the line it replaced. A month as the bound: the presets stop at a week, and a status that far ahead is a profile field, not a status.

**The shell's bound moves from 259 to 260 KB.** The conversation's profile panel and heading are in the first page, and drawing the emoji, the words and *Until 13:00*, gone at the end, takes them from 258.99 KB (with D-329’s timed mute, whose sidebar part was moved into the deferred chunk to fit) to 259.26 KB, measured file by file. One KB is the margin for a feature or two, as D-306 and D-319 raised it; the 400 KB backstop and the module bounds stay. Nothing else of this change is in the first page: the settings and their presets are deferred.

**Cost of reversing.** Two nullable columns and two optional fields: dropping them makes every line one kept for good, as it was.
